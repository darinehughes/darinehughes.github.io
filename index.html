04 / OBSERVATION & REFLECTION

DebriefScape

Turn recorded moments into a useful debrief.

Review video, add tags along a timeline, and explore patterns in an observation session. Custom categories and checklists support different coaching questions.

Our role Ongoing development and technical support for this collaborative research tool.

The local-first workflow reads video and session files from a folder you select, and writes annotations locally. The public site and integrations have their own access flows.

05 / FROM THE WORKSHOP

In development

Pantheon.

A small world. A long story.

Our passion project: an evolving god-simulation game about guiding island communities and caring for the lives within them.

Built in Godot, the current development build includes village work and construction, families and lasting histories, supply voyages, and Moss. Our longer-term ambition is a richer world shaped by the player’s care.
